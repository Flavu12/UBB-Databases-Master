DECODE THE WORD

What does play mean?

A tiny word with a surprisingly big range of meanings.

VERB

To have fun.

To take part in an activity for enjoyment, especially a game.

VERB

To perform.

To produce music, act as a character, or start a recording.

NOUN

A performance.

A story written to be performed on stage — a theatrical play.

WORD ORIGIN

Old English → Today

The verb traces back to Old English plegan / plegian, associated with movement, amusement and music. The noun relates to plega, meaning recreation or active movement. Over time, the word expanded into many different contexts.
